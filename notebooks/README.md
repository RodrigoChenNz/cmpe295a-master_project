# notebooks

Exploration and training runs — EDA, forecaster comparison, RL training curves, SHAP analysis.

Anything that becomes a result in the paper gets promoted out of here into a service or a
seeded script. Notebooks are where you figure it out, not where it lives.

Clear outputs before committing (`nbstripout` or Cell > All Output > Clear) — committed output
blobs make diffs unreadable and inflate the repo.
